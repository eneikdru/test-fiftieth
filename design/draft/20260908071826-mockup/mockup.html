# Test-Fiftieth: Feature 16 - "Holographic Presence" ## Overview The final piece of the Test-Fiftieth "Invisible Utility" refresh is the **Holographic Presence** system. Building on the Neural Engine 5.0 and Focus-Sphere, this feature moves communication from flat screens into the user's physical space using Fluidic 3.0 depth. ## Core Concepts - **Volumetric Projection:** Avatars and shared workspaces are projected with physical weight and Z-axis depth. - **Ambient Presence:** When a contact is "available," a subtle light refraction appears in the room's periphery, rather than a notification bubble. - **Haptic Echo:** Uses Surface-Morphing technology to simulate the tactile feel of objects being "passed" through the holographic window. ## Visual Language: Fluidic 3.0 - **Primary Aesthetic:** Frosted glass, chromatic aberration, and "light-leak" highlights. - **Color Palette:** Reactive "Ambient Sensing" colors (Sunset Gold to Deep Midnight). - **Typography:** Variable fonts that shift weight based on the user's eye-track focus. ## Interface Elements 1. **The Presence Ring:** A floating, glass-morphic controller for managing holographic callers. 2. **Focus-Sphere Privacy Mask:** Visual indicators showing when the camera is contextualizing the room to protect privacy. 3. **Z-Axis Workspace:** A layered interface where windows have actual physical distance between them.
